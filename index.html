How I design AI people can trust

Show why

Every AI decision comes with the reason behind it, in words the user already uses.

Keep people in charge

The AI suggests. People decide, and the interface makes that line obvious.

Make undo cheap

If an agent can change something, reversing it takes one step.

Be honest about limits

Label what the AI made, and say when it isn't sure.
